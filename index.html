The problem it solves

Every drip kit box promises a plant count. None of them mention that a 1/4-inch line gives up after about 30 feet, that 200 feet of 1/2-inch poly caps near 220 GPH, or that your "20 plants" need 11 gallons a week each in July - which is a 90-minute session, three times a week, through the right number of emitters. Emittermath does those four computations in order and labels every rule of thumb as a rule of thumb.

Gallons first

Canopy area and weekly ET become gallons per plant per day, with the exact 0.623 gal per sq ft per inch conversion.

Real zone budgets

Your zone demand is checked against your measured hose-bib flow at the 75% design rule, not the kit's optimism.

Honest labels

Unit conversions are exact; lateral limits and design fractions are labeled manufacturer and extension guidance.
